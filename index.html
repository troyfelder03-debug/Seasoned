📱 Install on iPhone

Open this site in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen. Open it once while online so the offline cache can finish.

Automatic saving: every inventory change is saved on this device. The app also keeps a few local recovery snapshots. External backups are still important because clearing Safari website data or resetting the phone can remove local data.
